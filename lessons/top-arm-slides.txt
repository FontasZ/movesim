Lie on your left side, knees bent, one on the other, your head on a pillow. The left arm lies forward on the floor; the right hand rests on the floor in front of your chest.

Slide the right hand forward along the floor, past the left hand, so that the right shoulder blade comes forward. Come back. Several times.

Now lift the right hand and take the arm back, open, toward the ceiling and behind you, letting the chest turn. Only as far as is easy. Come back.

Rest on your side.

Slide the right hand forward again, and notice whether it goes further now.
