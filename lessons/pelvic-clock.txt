Lie on your back, knees bent, feet standing comfortably apart. Imagine that the back of your pelvis lies on the face of a clock: 12 o'clock toward your head, 6 o'clock toward your feet, 3 o'clock at your left hip, 9 o'clock at your right hip.

Gently press the pelvis toward 12, so that your lower back comes toward the floor, and let it return. Several times, slowly, without effort.

Now toward 6: the tailbone presses a little and the lower back lifts away from the floor. Come back. Notice what your head does.

Go between 12 and 6, from one to the other, slowly.

Rest, with your legs long if you like.

Bend your knees again. Roll the pelvis toward 3, toward your left hip, and back. Then toward 9, your right hip, and back. Several times each.

Now the diagonals: toward half past one, between 12 and 3, and back; then toward half past seven, between 6 and 9.

Rest.

Now go around the clock: 12, 3, 6, 9, and back to 12, slowly, touching each hour. Several times. Then the other way around: 12, 9, 6, 3.

Rest, and notice how the back of your pelvis and your lower back lie on the floor now.
