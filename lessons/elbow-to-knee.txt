Lie on your back, knees bent, feet standing, arms long by your sides.

Lift your left knee a little toward you, so the foot leaves the floor, and put it back. Several times.

Now bring your right elbow toward the left knee as the knee comes toward you. Let the head lift a little with it. Come back down. Small, easy, several times.

Rest.

Do the same, and this time leave the head on the floor: only the elbow and the knee come toward each other.

Rest. Then do the whole diagonal once more, with the head, and notice how far the elbow and the knee come now.
