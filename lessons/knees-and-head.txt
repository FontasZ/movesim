Lie on your back and bend your knees so the feet stand on the floor. Notice how your back meets the floor: where it presses, where it stays away.

Slowly let both knees go a little to the right, and come back to the middle. Do it several times, small and easy, without effort. Notice which parts of your back come along.

Rest. Let the legs stand without holding them.

Now let the knees go to the left and come back. Is it the same as to the right?

Let the knees go to the right and leave them there. Now roll your head to the left, and back to the middle. Several times. Then bring the knees back to the middle.

Rest.

Press lightly through the right foot, so that the right knee goes forward a little, away from you. Notice how the right side of the pelvis lifts. Do it a few times.

Rest, and notice how your back lies on the floor now.
