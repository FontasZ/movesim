Sit on the front half of a chair, feet standing on the floor.

Turn your head to the right, as if to look behind you, and come back. Notice how far you see.

Now turn the chest to the right as well, and let the head go with it. Come back. Several times.

Turn the chest to the right and the head to the left, at the same time. Small. Come back.

Now leave the head facing forward and turn only the chest to the right, and back.

Rest. Then turn head and chest together to the right once more, and notice how far you see behind you now.
