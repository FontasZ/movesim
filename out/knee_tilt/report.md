# Knees to the right: what the skeleton did

## In plain words

- The pelvis rolled 8.8° to the right (to the right, as expected).
- The twist faded going up the spine: by T7_8 it was under a tenth of the pelvis roll.
- The ribcage rolled 0.01° and the skull at most 0.02°.
- Back pressure moved 48 mm to the right: the left side of the back unloaded by 140 N and the right side took +221 N.
- The feet stayed planted: no point of contact slid more than 4.2 mm in total.
- The feet rolled onto their edges: ballout_L, ballin_R came off the floor at the peak.
- After returning, the pelvis was -0.01° from where it started.
