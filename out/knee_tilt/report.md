# Knees to the right: what the skeleton did

Lying on the back, knees bent, feet flat, arms by the sides. A guide's hands moved both knees 20° to the right over 3 s, held for 1 s, and brought them back over 3 s. Everything else was passive. At the peak (t = 4.8 s) the knees had tipped 19.2° (the hands asked for 20.0°).

## In plain words

- The pelvis rolled 8.3° to the right (to the right, as expected).
- The twist faded going up the spine: under half the pelvis roll by T11, under a tenth by T8.
- T6, in the middle of the chest, rolled 0.10°; the skull at most 0.04°.
- Back pressure moved 44 mm to the right: the left side of the back unloaded by 158 N and the right side took +191 N.
- The left side of the back lifted: glute_L 19 mm, L5_back_L 11 mm, L4_back_L 11 mm, L3_back_L 10 mm, L2_back_L 8 mm, L1_back_L 6 mm.
- Off the floor at the peak: glute_L, met5_L, midfoot_L, met1_R.
- Newly touching the floor at the peak: T9_back_R.
- The feet slid: up to 5.1 mm in total.
- After returning, the pelvis was -0.01° from where it started.
