# Knees to the right: what the skeleton did

## In plain words

- The pelvis rolled 8.5° to the right (to the right, as expected).
- The twist faded going up the spine: under half the pelvis roll by T12, under a tenth by T9.
- T6, in the middle of the chest, rolled 0.11°; the skull at most 0.07°.
- Back pressure moved 43 mm to the right: the left side of the back unloaded by 159 N and the right side took +181 N.
- The left side of the back lifted: glute_L 20 mm, L5_back_L 12 mm, L4_back_L 11 mm, L3_back_L 10 mm, L2_back_L 8 mm, L1_back_L 6 mm.
- Off the floor at the peak: glute_L, met5_L, midfoot_L, met1_R, met5_R.
- Newly touching the floor at the peak: T9_back_R.
- The feet stayed planted: no point of contact slid more than 0.5 mm in total.
- After returning, the pelvis was -0.02° from where it started.
