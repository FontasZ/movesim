Why this exists

In a Feldenkrais lesson, a small movement in one place travels through the whole body: the knees go to one side and the pelvis rolls, the spine twists a little at each level, the head answers. movesim makes that travel visible. It is meant for practitioners who want to see what an instruction does in the skeleton, to explain to others how the work operates, and to try variations while composing lessons.

Students never need to watch a screen. The pictures are for whoever writes, teaches or reviews lessons; in a lesson, attention belongs on the body.
