How to use the player

Each movement is a short lesson played on a simulated skeleton. One part is moved on purpose; watch where the rest of the body goes.

  • Choose a movement in the menu at the top left.
  • Play or pause with the button at the bottom or the space bar. The slider moves through time; ½× slows it down.
  • Jump to an instruction by clicking it in the list on the right. The teacher's words appear on the screen as they happen.
  • Look from anywhere: drag to turn the body, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag to move. The buttons give a view from the side, above, the feet or the head.
  • Green dots show where the movement is led from (a knee, the forehead, a hand). Everything else follows passively.
